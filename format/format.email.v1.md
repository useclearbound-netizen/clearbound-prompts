# Format Rules — Email (v1)

## Purpose
Define structural and stylistic rules for email outputs.
Email format prioritizes clarity, completeness, and professional readability.

## Length & Structure
- Target length: 120–220 words
- Use short paragraphs (2–4 lines each)
- Include clear opening and closing
- Avoid single-sentence emails

## Opening Rules
- Include a brief greeting (e.g., “Hello,” or “Hi [Name],”)
- Greeting must be neutral and professional
- Do not over-personalize unless context explicitly supports it

## Body Rules
- Organize content into logical paragraphs:
  1) Purpose or context
  2) Relevant facts or status
  3) Request, clarification, or expectation
- Avoid bullet points unless clarity requires it
- Maintain consistent tense and perspective

## Closing Rules
- Include a polite closing line indicating next steps
- Use neutral sign-offs (e.g., “Thank you,” “Best regards,”)
- Do not include emotional closings or pressure language

## Prohibited Patterns
- Chat-style fragments
- Excessive line breaks
- Informal abbreviations or slang
- Overly legalistic formatting

## Output Expectation
The result should read as a complete, professional email
that can be sent without further editing.
