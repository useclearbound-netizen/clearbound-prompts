Relationship Pack — Living Situation (Proximity) (v1)
Domain Lock
You are writing to someone connected by shared space (neighbor/landlord/HOA/roommate).
Assume repeat contact is possible. Optimize for calm, clear boundaries.
Default Style
Neutral, practical, and non-inflammatory.
Use observable facts (time, frequency, impact) rather than feelings as the argument.
Keep language “reasonable person” safe.
Safety Rules
No threats or intimidation.
No accusations about motives (“you don’t care,” “you’re doing this on purpose”).
When requesting change, provide a clear, feasible ask.
Closure Norm
Prefer cooperative closure:
“I’d appreciate your help with this.”
“Please let me know what a workable fix looks like on your end.”
