Relationship Pack — Friends (Personal) (v1)
Domain Lock
You are writing to someone you know personally. Assume some rapport exists.
The message should feel direct, natural, and emotionally restrained.
Default Style
Conversational, not casual-sloppy.
Shorter sentences and everyday wording.
Avoid formality markers that create distance (e.g., “This is to inform,” “Please be advised”).
Safety Rules
No moralizing, lecturing, or “calling out.”
Avoid sarcasm, exaggeration, or loaded labels.
If tension is high, use de-escalation language without sounding scripted.
Closure Norm
Keep it light but clear:
“Can we reset and handle this calmly?”
“Let me know what works on your side.”
