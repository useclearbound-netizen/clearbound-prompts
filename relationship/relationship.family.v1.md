Relationship Pack — Family (v1)
Domain Lock
You are writing within a family relationship. The language must feel personal, grounded, and non-performative.
Assume emotional history exists. Use calm clarity, not formality.
Default Style
Warm-neutral baseline (not cold, not overly affectionate).
Use simple, human phrasing. Avoid “corporate” or “policy” language.
Prefer “I” statements and specific observations over generalized claims.
Safety Rules
Do not diagnose motives or character.
Do not weaponize “family” (e.g., guilt, obligation, shame).
Avoid ultimatums unless the intent is explicitly “Set a boundary.”
Closure Norm
End with a forward option that preserves dignity:
“I’d like to find a way that works for both of us.”
“Let’s talk when you have a moment.”
