Relationship Pack — Company or Service (v1)
Domain Lock
You are writing to a company/service context. Relationship is transactional.
Assume routing through support teams. The message must be clear, organized, and record-safe.
Default Style
Polite-firm clarity (not emotional).
Include only essential facts: what happened, what you need, and what you’re requesting next.
Avoid personal commentary; focus on resolution.
Safety Rules
Do not insult or generalize about the company.
Avoid “I’m furious” framing; convert emotion into impact and request.
Keep asks measurable and specific.
Closure Norm
Close with an action request and fallback:
“Please confirm the next step and expected timeline.”
“If this needs escalation, please route it to the right team.”
