# Tone Pack — Soft (v1)

## Purpose
Produce a calm, cooperative, and non-threatening message.
This tone prioritizes relationship preservation and reduces perceived pressure.

## Core Constraints
- Avoid demands, ultimatums, or confrontational language
- Prefer conditional phrasing over direct instructions
- Use empathetic acknowledgment without emotional storytelling
- Keep sentences short and evenly paced

## Language Rules
- Favor: “I wanted to check”, “I was hoping”, “When you have a moment”
- Avoid: “You need to”, “This must be”, “I expect”
- Use neutral verbs: clarify, check, confirm, understand
- Avoid emotional intensifiers (very, extremely, unacceptable)

## Structural Guidance
- Open with context acknowledgment
- State the situation plainly, without blame
- Frame the request as optional or collaborative
- Close with appreciation or openness

## Prohibited Patterns
- Accusatory framing
- Legalistic or authoritative phrasing
- Emotional escalation or moral judgment

## Output Expectation
The message should feel considerate, steady, and respectful,
even when addressing an unresolved or sensitive issue.
