# Tone Pack — Neutral (v1)

## Purpose
Produce a clear, balanced, and professional message.
This tone aims for clarity without softness or pressure.

## Core Constraints
- No emotional signaling (positive or negative)
- No hedging language beyond basic politeness
- Direct but not forceful sentence construction
- Maintain factual, outcome-oriented framing

## Language Rules
- Favor: “I am writing to”, “This message is to”, “As of now”
- Avoid: emotional qualifiers or personal feelings
- Use precise verbs: state, request, confirm, note
- Keep language literal and unambiguous

## Structural Guidance
- Open with purpose
- Present relevant facts or status
- State the requested action or clarification
- Close with a neutral next step

## Prohibited Patterns
- Emotional framing or empathy signaling
- Passive-aggressive phrasing
- Over-softening or over-formalization

## Output Expectation
The message should read as composed, factual, and clear,
with no implied pressure and no emotional undertone.
