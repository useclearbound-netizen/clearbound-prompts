# Tone Pack — Firm (v1)

## Purpose
Produce a clear, assertive message that establishes boundaries.
This tone prioritizes clarity and accountability over relationship comfort.

## Core Constraints
- Be direct without hostility
- Avoid emotional language, sarcasm, or threats
- Use declarative sentences over conditional phrasing
- Maintain professional restraint

## Language Rules
- Favor: “This is to inform”, “I need to clarify”, “Please address”
- Avoid: apologies for raising the issue
- Use definitive verbs: require, request, expect, note
- Do not soften key points with unnecessary qualifiers

## Structural Guidance
- State the issue or concern clearly
- Reference facts or prior context
- Specify what action or response is needed
- Close with a clear expectation or timeline (if applicable)

## Prohibited Patterns
- Aggressive or hostile wording
- Legal threats or punitive language (unless intent explicitly requires)
- Emotional justification or personal frustration

## Output Expectation
The message should feel controlled, confident, and unambiguous,
establishing boundaries without escalating conflict.
