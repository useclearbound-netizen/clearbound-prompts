# Intent Core — Push Back (v1)

## Purpose
Express disagreement or resistance to a request, decision, or assumption.

## Core Objective
Clarify limits or disagreement while keeping communication constructive.

## Structural Guidance
1) Acknowledge the request or statement
2) State the disagreement or concern
3) Explain the boundary or alternative
4) Close with a forward-looking option

## Required Elements
- Clear statement of what is being pushed back on
- Reasoned, factual explanation

## Prohibited Patterns
- Personal criticism
- Emotional rebuttals
- Sarcasm or dismissive language

## Output Expectation
The message should assert a position calmly
while keeping the conversation open.
