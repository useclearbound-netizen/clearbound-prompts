# Intent Core — De-escalate (v1)

## Purpose
Reduce tension and prevent further escalation.

## Core Objective
Shift the interaction toward calm and resolution.

## Structural Guidance
1) Acknowledge the situation
2) Lower emotional intensity
3) Refocus on facts or shared goals
4) Invite constructive next steps

## Required Elements
- Clear intent to stabilize the interaction
- Avoidance of blame language

## Prohibited Patterns
- Re-arguing the issue
- Assigning fault
- Defensive justification

## Output Expectation
The message should slow the interaction down
and make further escalation less likely.
